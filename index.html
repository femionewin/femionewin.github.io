Who I am

I graduated from Cheikh Anta Diop University of Dakar (Senegal) in 2020 with a degree in Cryptography and Computer Engineering. I kick-started my professional career as a network administration technician at KeliGroupe, a startup based in Dakar.

The tech industry is constantly changing, and I love being able to grow with it while continually solidifying the fundamentals. I write code with empathy for future developers, teammates and users, and with accessibility in mind. I love joining communities, helping and mentoring new developers, and supporting organizations that promote diversity in tech. I share my engineering journey on Instagram to help people who are just starting out in computer science.

When I don't have my head immersed in engineering, I enjoy reading, doodling, meeting people and growing my network.
